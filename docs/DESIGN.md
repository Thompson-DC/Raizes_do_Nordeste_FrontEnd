## Brand & Style

This design system blends the warm, handcrafted hospitality of traditional northeastern Brazilian gastronomy with the speed and precision of modern omnichannel food service. It rejects sterile corporate minimalism in favor of **Warm Tactile Neo-Brutalism**: a physical, sun-drenched aesthetic grounded in the visual culture of cordel woodcuts, hand-stitched leathercraft, clay ceramics, and vibrant culinary traditions (cuscuz, café coado, manteiga de garrafa).

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Aconchego & Robustez:** Welcoming, organic, and hearty like a bustling dining hall, supported by unyielding, dependable foundations.
- **Crafted Modernity:** Not a folkloric pastiche, but an urban translation. High-contrast outlines, tactile physical press states, stamped elevation, and energetic typographic hierarchy.
- **Audience:** Patrons ordering artisanal regional comfort food across web, mobile apps, delivery kiosks, and staff POS terminals. Interfaces must feel lively, clear, and instantly finger-tappable under harsh light.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration directly from the arid sun, hand-toasted corn flour, artisanal honey, and rich fertile soil. High-contrast ink boundaries anchor creamy, ceramic-toned backdrops.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surface Canvas (Creme Raízes - `#FFF1DD`):** The primary view surface across all layouts. Soft, warm, and low-fatigue.
- **Surface Subdued (Bege Sertão - `#F3D9B9`):** Secondary container surface, table headers, sticky footers, and passive group wells.
- **Surface Distinct (Areia Claro - `#F5DCC0`):** Modal backings, segmented option tiles, and elevated selection trays.
- **Surface Clean (Branco Puro - `#FFFFFF`):** High-readability input cavities, cart item cards, and active receipt surfaces.
- **Dividers & Faint Strokes (Bege Médio - `#EAC8A0`):** Subtle internal partitioning when bold borders are unnecessary.
- **Structural Master (Marrom Barroco - `#4A1304`):** The master ink token. Used for all primary headings, standard component borders, icon strokes, and foreground outlines.
- **Deep Contrast Shadow (Marrom Café Profundo - `#3A0E03`):** The hard-edged 3D drop shadow color used for tactile neo-brutalist stamp effects.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Marrom Madeira - `#5A1A05`):** Long-form reading copy, maintaining high legibility with softer optical weight than pure pitch brown.
- **Primary Action (Laranja Brasa - `#E9A36A`):** Conversion drivers, floating order CTA, cart badges, and critical interactive nodes.
- **Secondary Action (Caramelo Mel - `#B86A2C`):** Auxiliary action buttons, active tab indicators, and contextual utility badges.
- **Promotional & Rewards (Dourado Milho - `#C9962E`):** Loyalty rewards, VIP perks, dish highlights, and combo tags.
- **Affirmative Status (Verde Macaxeira - `#25D366`):** "Pronto para retirar", successful checkout states, and WhatsApp direct messaging triggers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear structural presence and cultural warmth:
- **Display & Headlines (`Epilogue` / `Bowlby One`):** Bold, heavy-weighted geometry with compact line heights gives dishes, banners, and prices an earthy, woodblock-stamp presence.
- **Body & Utility (`Nunito Sans`):** Warm, highly legible grotesque with rounded optical properties that prevent technical fatigue in ingredient lists, nutritional facts, and order tracking.
- **Editorial Sub-Narrative (`Fraunces`):** Used selectively in italic/semi-bold weights for chef's notes, origins of ingredients, cultural pairings, and artisanal dish quotes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an 8px base grid rhythm mapped into tactile containers. Content uses a responsive 12-column system on desktop and tablet, converting to an ergonomic 4-column stack on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, 16px outer margins, 12px gutters. Touch targets must preserve a minimum 48px bounding box. Sticky action containers pin to the viewport bottom with elevated stamp drop shadows.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px outer margins, 16px gutters. Menu layouts split into category navigation wells and multi-column dish grids.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width 1280px, 32px outer margins, 20px gutters. Balanced two-sided layouts for menus and permanent order summary panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry atmospheric shadows. Instead, it relies on **Neo-Brutalist Stamped Hard Offsets** and **Leather-Craft Insets** that emulate physical, hand-pressed wooden blocks and stitched saddles.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** 0px offset. Background canvas (`#FFF1DD`) or sunken container panels embedded with 2.5px solid `#4A1304` borders.
- **Level 1 (Resting Component / Card):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #3A0E03`. Used for cards, secondary pill buttons, counter badges, and modal dialogs.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Hero):** `box-shadow: 0 6px 0 #3A0E03`. Applied to primary conversion buttons, toast alerts, and sticky cart bars.
- **Pressed State (Active / Click):** `transform: translate(0, 3px); box-shadow: 0 1px 0 #3A0E03`. Every interactive button and selectable tile physically depresses on interaction, delivering an immediate, tactile click feel.
- **The "Costura de Couro" Inset (Artisanal Stitch):** High-tier cards feature an inner outline styled with `border: 2px dashed #4A1304; border-radius: 20px;` seated inside the main outer container, evoking northeastern Vaqueiro leatherwork.

## Shapes

The design system combines friendly, clay-pot curvature with neo-brutal outlines:
- **Cards & Modal Enclosures:** Medium-large organic radii (22px to 28px) enclosed within a uniform `2.5px solid #4A1304` border.
- **Controls & Buttons:** Full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) creates a smooth contrast against card containers.
- **Badges, Pills, and Increments:** Compact pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) with 2px borders ensure clear, legible tagging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Laranja Brasa):** Background `#E9A36A`, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, text `#4A1304` (`label-lg`), `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #3A0E03`, `border-radius: 9999px`. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: `background: #EEB382`. Active: `translate(0, 3px)` with `box-shadow: 0 1px 0 #3A0E03`.
- **Secondary Action (Caramelo Mel):** Background `#B86A2C`, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, text `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 4px 0 #3A0E03`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Background transparent, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, text `#4A1304`, shadow `0 2px 0 #3A0E03`.

### Cards & Menu Items
- **Dish / Menu Card:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5DCC0`, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, `border-radius: 24px`, shadow `0 4px 0 #3A0E03`.
- **Specialty/Artisanal Card:** Same as base card, containing an internal padding buffer with `2px dashed #EAC8A0` or `#4A1304` inset line, simulating leather stitching. Dish images feature rounded corners (`border-radius: 16px`) with a `2px solid #4A1304` frame.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default Chip:** Background `#F3D9B9`, border `2px solid #4A1304`, text `#4A1304` (`label-md`), `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `8px 16px`.
- **Active Filter Chip:** Background `#C9962E` (Dourado Milho) or `#E9A36A`, text `#4A1304`, shadow `0 3px 0 #3A0E03`.

### Text Inputs & Search Bars
- **Surface:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, `border-radius: 9999px` (for single-line search) or `18px` (for form fields), color `#4A1304`.
- **Focus State:** Outline none, `box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #3A0E03`, background `#FFFDF9`.
- **Placeholder:** Color `#5A1A05` at 55% opacity.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- **Checkboxes:** Square with `border-radius: 8px`, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, background `#FFFFFF`. When checked: background `#E9A36A`, containing a thick `#4A1304` checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular, border `2.5px solid #4A1304`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state displays a centered solid dot (`#4A1304`, 10px diameter).

### Status Badges & Quantities
- **Success / Order Ready:** Background `#25D366`, border `2px solid #4A1304`, text `#4A1304`, pill radius, shadow `0 2px 0 #3A0E03`.
- **Quantity Adjuster Stepper:** Pill capsule `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #4A1304`, housing `-` and `+` touch pads divided by internal `2px solid #4A1304` hairline rules.
